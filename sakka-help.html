Sakka: Baloot & Trix Score Help

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email us.

Which games does Sakka score?

Baloot, Hand, Tarneeb, Trix and Kout. Baloot and Hand are free with every feature. Tarneeb, Trix and Kout need Sakka Pro.

How do I start a match?

On Home, tap New match. Pick a game, set the team or player names and the target, then tap Start match. Names you used before show up as suggestions. The home screen shows the match in progress so you can continue it.

Quick or Calculator?

Tap Add round to open the round sheet. Quick works like the paper score sheet: tap a box, then type its points. Calculator applies the rules of the game for you. In Baloot you pick Sun or Hokm, the buyer, the card points, any projects, and doubling. Sakka shows the game points before you save the round.

How do I fix a mistake?

Tap a round in the list to edit it. Swipe a round to delete it, and tap Undo if you deleted the wrong one.

What happens when a team reaches the target?

The match ends and shows the winner. Tap Play again to keep the names and count series wins (sakkas) for each side.

Where is my match history?

Open History. Without Sakka Pro it keeps the last 3 finished matches. Sakka Pro keeps all of them and adds stats for every team and player.

Does the screen stay on?

Sakka keeps the screen awake while an unfinished match is open, so you do not have to unlock your phone between rounds.

Does Sakka work in Arabic?

Yes. The app has a right-to-left layout and reads and shows Arabic-Indic digits. Open Settings and tap Language to pick one of 48 languages.

What does Sakka Pro unlock?

Tarneeb, Trix and Kout, unlimited match history, stats for every team and player, and 6 more themes. It is one payment with no subscription. Open Settings, then tap Get Sakka Pro to see the price in your currency.

I bought Sakka Pro on another phone

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Pro unlocks on this phone at no extra cost.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes your matches. See the privacy policy.
