Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your phone. Nameo: Name Day Calendar collects nothing.

Last updated 2026-10-23

What the app stores

Everything you enter is saved in the app's own storage on your device. The app has no account and no server. Your data is never uploaded.

What the app does not do

  • No data is collected by the developer or by anyone else.
  • No analytics and no crash reporting.
  • No tracking and no advertising. The app shows no ads.
  • No third-party services receive any information from the app.

Contacts

Importing from Contacts is optional and part of Nameo Pro. The app asks for access only when you tap Import. It reads first names on your phone to find their name days. It keeps only the first names you confirm. Your contacts, phone numbers and emails are never stored or uploaded.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server sends them and nobody else learns who you follow.

Purchases

Nameo Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. The developer never sees your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only learns whether you own Nameo Pro.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Because the data never leaves your phone, there is nothing to request from the developer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
