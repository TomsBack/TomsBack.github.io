SimplePrintablePDF Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

SimplePrintablePDF uses an internal publishing tool to create Pins for digital printable listings owned by the app operator.

Data used by the app

The app may use listing titles, descriptions, product images, destination links, board IDs, and Pin metadata needed to publish content.

Account access

The app uses OAuth only to access boards and Pins authorized by the account owner. It does not request access to unrelated account data, ads, or payment information.

Data storage

OAuth tokens and publishing logs are stored locally by the app operator. They are not sold, shared, or exposed to third parties.

Data sharing

The app sends Pin content and related metadata to the publishing platform only when publishing or managing Pins through its official API.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked from account settings or by deleting the locally stored OAuth token.
