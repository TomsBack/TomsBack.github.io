Privacy Policy

Your data stays in your browser. LeanTabs, a Firefox add-on, collects nothing.

Last updated 2026-11-17

What LeanTabs does not do

  • No data is collected by the developer or by anyone else.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no ads.
  • No account and no server.

What LeanTabs stores

These are saved in Firefox's storage for the add-on, on your device:

  • Your settings and site lists.
  • Counters: tabs unloaded, embeds held back and estimated memory freed.
  • Whether you dismissed the two banners in the popup.
  • Which add-ons from LeanTabs' own list you have, if you allow it.

What LeanTabs reads on pages

Before it unloads a tab, LeanTabs counts the tab's elements, requests, frames and videos, and checks whether a text field was edited. It never reads password fields and never stores page text.

Your add-on list

"Find my add-ons" is optional. Firefox asks for permission first. LeanTabs then reads your add-on list, keeps only the add-ons it works with, and never sends the list anywhere.

Requests to other sites

  • YouTube preview images: a held-back YouTube video shows its preview image, which loads from YouTube's image server. You can turn this off in settings.
  • Text-only editions: if you turn this on, CNN and NPR articles open on lite.cnn.com and text.npr.org. It is off by default.
  • An embed you click to load connects to its own site, as it would without LeanTabs.

Deleting your data

Removing LeanTabs from Firefox deletes everything it stored. Because the data never leaves your browser, there is nothing to request from the developer.
