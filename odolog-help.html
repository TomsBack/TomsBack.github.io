Odolog: Mileage Logbook Help

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email us.

How do I add a trip?

On Trips, tap Add trip (the plus button). Pick the date, the vehicle and a purpose, then add the odometer start and end, or just the distance. Tap Save trip. The odometer start fills in from your last trip. Round trip doubles the distance. Duplicate copies a trip.

Odometer or distance?

Use the odometer when you want readings in your log, since some countries ask for them. Use distance when you only know how far you drove. Odolog warns you when an end reading is below the start or below an earlier trip.

How do I record a trip with GPS?

Tap Record a drive, or Record trip at the top of Trips, then tap Start trip. Odolog asks to use your location While Using the app. A banner shows the trip while it runs, and the blue location indicator shows on your phone. Tap Stop and save. The trip sheet opens with the distance. Check it and tap Save trip. Odolog never starts a trip on its own.

What does Odolog keep from a recorded trip?

The start time, the end time and the distance. The route is not stored. GPS runs only while a trip is recording.

How do vehicles work?

The version without Pro has 1 vehicle. Odolog Pro adds as many as you need. Each vehicle has a name, a plate, a distance unit and, for some countries, a rate class. Archive a vehicle you no longer drive. Its trips stay in your log.

Where do the rates come from?

Odolog has official per-distance rates for 18 countries: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, Germany, Austria, France, Netherlands, Belgium, Spain, Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland, Poland and Czechia. Switzerland and Italy have no single official rate, so Odolog shows no estimate there. Each rate shows its source and the date we checked it. Each trip uses the rate in force on its day, in your country's tax year. When a new rate is not published yet, the summary says the estimate is provisional. The summary is an estimate. Odolog does not promise that any tax authority accepts the log. Check with your tax advisor.

How do I export my log?

Open Summary and tap Export CSV or PDF. Choose the vehicle and the period, then tap Share PDF logbook or Share CSV. Sharing the files needs Odolog Pro.

How do fuel and costs work?

Open the Costs tab. Tap Add fill-up to log the date, odometer, amount of fuel and total price. Switch on Filled to full for each full tank. Fuel economy is measured from one full tank to the next, so it appears after two full fill-ups with odometer readings. Tap Add expense for tolls, parking, maintenance, insurance, tax and other costs. Choose the fuel unit and the currency in Fuel and costs. The overview shows the total cost and the cost per distance for each vehicle and year.

Can I keep receipt photos?

Yes. In an expense, tap Take photo or Choose photo. Odolog opens your photos or camera only then. The photo stays on this phone and is not part of CSV or PDF exports.

How do maintenance reminders work?

On the Costs tab, in Reminders, tap Add reminder. Set a distance, a number of months or both. The reminder comes at whichever is first. When it is due by date, Odolog sends a notification on that day. When it is due by odometer, a banner shows in the app. Notifications are local. Nothing is sent from a server.

Places, clients and claims

In Settings, tap Places and clients to save places and add clients or projects. The trip form suggests saved places and fills the purpose from your last trip between them. Tag a trip with a client, then set its claim status to Not claimed, Claimed or Paid. On the Trips tab, filter by vehicle, client, status, purpose and period. With a filter on, the Update claims bar marks every listed trip at once. Summary, Export CSV or PDF includes the client, places and status, and Client report (PDF) makes a report for one client.

Live Activity, widgets and Siri

While a GPS trip records, Odolog shows its distance and time on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with a Stop button. Turn it on or off in Settings, Widgets and Siri. The widgets show this month's distance and estimate, your last trip and a Start trip button. Say "Start a trip in Odolog", "Stop the trip in Odolog" or "Log a trip in Odolog" to Siri. The phrases also appear in the Shortcuts app.

What does Odolog Pro add?

Unlimited trips, vehicles and expenses, receipt photos, maintenance reminders, the cost overview, clients, claims, the client report, widgets, CSV and PDF export and more themes. The version without Pro has 1 vehicle, 20 trips and 3 expenses each month, the fuel log, places, Live Activity and Siri. Open Settings, then tap Get Odolog Pro to see it in your currency.

I bought Odolog Pro on another phone

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Pro unlocks on this phone at no extra cost.

Can I change the language?

The app follows your phone's language. To pick another one, open Settings and tap Language. The app is available in 48 languages.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.
