Odolog: Mileage Logbook Help

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email us.

How do I add a trip?

On Trips, tap Add trip (the plus button). Pick the date, the vehicle and a purpose, then add the odometer start and end, or just the distance. Tap Save trip. The odometer start fills in from your last trip. Round trip doubles the distance. Duplicate copies a trip.

Odometer or distance?

Use the odometer when you want readings in your log, since some countries ask for them. Use distance when you only know how far you drove. Odolog warns you when an end reading is below the start or below an earlier trip.

How do I record a trip with GPS?

Tap Record a drive, or Record trip at the top of Trips, then tap Start trip. Odolog asks to use your location While Using the app. A banner shows the trip while it runs, and the blue location indicator shows on your phone. Tap Stop and save. The trip sheet opens with the distance. Check it and tap Save trip. Odolog never starts a trip on its own.

What does Odolog keep from a recorded trip?

The start time, the end time and the distance. The route is not stored. GPS runs only while a trip is recording.

How do vehicles work?

The free version has 1 vehicle. Odolog Pro adds as many as you need. Each vehicle has a name, a plate, a distance unit and, for some countries, a rate class. Archive a vehicle you no longer drive. Its trips stay in your log.

Where do the rates come from?

Odolog has official per-distance rates for 18 countries: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, Germany, Austria, France, Netherlands, Belgium, Spain, Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland, Poland and Czechia. Switzerland and Italy have no single official rate, so Odolog shows no estimate there. Each rate shows its source and the date we checked it. Each trip uses the rate in force on its day, in your country's tax year. When a new rate is not published yet, the summary says the estimate is provisional. The summary is an estimate. Odolog does not promise that any tax authority accepts the log. Check with your tax advisor.

How do I export my log?

Open Summary and tap Export CSV or PDF. Choose the vehicle and the period, then tap Share PDF logbook or Share CSV. Sharing the files needs Odolog Pro.

What does Odolog Pro unlock?

Unlimited trips, unlimited vehicles, CSV and PDF export and more themes, with one payment. The free version has 1 vehicle and 20 trips each month. There is no subscription. Open Settings, then tap Get Odolog Pro to see the price in your currency.

I bought Odolog Pro on another phone

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Pro unlocks on this phone at no extra cost.

Can I change the language?

The app follows your phone's language. To pick another one, open Settings and tap Language. The app is available in 48 languages.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.
