Privacy Policy

Photo Compressor does not collect your photos. Your photos never leave your device.

Last updated 12 November 2026

What the app collects

None of your photos or personal details. The app has no account, no server of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer never receives your photos or any information about you. On Android the app does not use the advertising ID.

Two things can happen without a tap:

  • Update check, iPhone only. When the app opens on iPhone, it asks Expo, the service that delivers app updates, whether a newer version exists. The request carries a random ID made for this install, the app version and the platform. It carries none of your photos. On Android the app makes no update checks and sends nothing.
  • Rating prompt. After your second completed save, the app may ask the App Store or Google Play to show its rating prompt, at most once per version. Your rating goes to that store.

Your photos

Photos are read and compressed on your device. They are never uploaded. The compressed copies stay on your device until you save them to Photos or Files, or share them yourself. On Android, they stay until you save them to the gallery or a folder, or share them.

The app never changes or deletes an original. Unsaved copies are kept in the app's temporary storage and are removed when you discard them or when the system clears it.

Photo library access

On iPhone, the app asks for access to your photo library so you can pick photos and save the compressed copies. You can allow all photos, only selected photos, or no access. Without access you can still import photos from Files. Change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

On Android, the app uses the system photo picker. It has no permission to read your photos and gets only the photos you pick. Saving to the gallery needs no permission on Android 10 and later. On Android 9 and older, the app asks for storage access the first time you save.

Location in photos

Photos can hold the GPS location where they were taken. Turn on Remove location and the app leaves GPS data out of the copies it makes. This happens on your device. Turn on Remove all metadata to also leave out the capture date, camera and lens.

On Android, the system photo picker may remove the location before the app gets the photo.

Settings

Your compression defaults, such as quality, dimensions and format, are saved in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app removes them, including the update check ID on iPhone.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the copies go to the app you choose. That app's privacy policy covers what happens next.

You can also share photos into the app from another app. The app gets only the photos you share.

Purchases

The app is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. There are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases. Payment is handled by Apple or Google. No payment details reach the app or the developer.

Children

The app collects no photos or personal details from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
