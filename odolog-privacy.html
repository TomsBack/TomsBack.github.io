Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your phone. Odolog: Mileage Logbook collects nothing.

Last updated 2026-11-03

What the app stores

Everything you enter is saved in the app's own storage on your device. The app has no account and no server. Your data is never uploaded.

Location

Odolog uses your location only while you record a trip that you started with the Start button. It uses the While Using permission, and the blue location indicator shows while it runs. Odolog never records a trip on its own. When you stop, only the distance is saved with the trip. The route is not stored. The location never leaves your phone.

Photos and camera

Odolog opens your photo library or the camera only when you add a receipt to an expense. The photo is copied into the app's own storage on your phone. It is never uploaded and it is not part of exports.

Notifications

If you allow them, Odolog sends a local notification on the due date of a maintenance reminder. They are scheduled on your phone. The app has no push service and no server.

Lock Screen, widgets and Siri

The Live Activity, the widgets and the Siri shortcuts show and use data that is already on your phone, such as this month's distance and your last trip. They share it with the app on the device only.

What the app does not do

  • No data is collected by the developer or by anyone else.
  • No analytics and no crash reporting.
  • No tracking and no advertising. The app shows no ads.
  • No third-party services receive any information from the app.

Purchases

Odolog Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. The developer never sees your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only learns whether you own Odolog Pro.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Because the data never leaves your phone, there is nothing to request from the developer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
