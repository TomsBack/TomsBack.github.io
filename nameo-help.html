Nameo: Name Day Calendar Help

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email us.

How do I choose my calendars?

Nameo picks the calendar of your phone's region the first time you open it. To change it, open Settings and tap Calendars. The free version shows 1 calendar. Nameo Pro shows more calendars side by side. Nameo has 15 calendars: Czechia, Slovakia, Poland, Hungary, Croatia, Slovenia, Denmark, Sweden (list of 2001), Finland (list of 2010), Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Bulgaria, Romania and Greece. Sweden and Finland use older editions whose protection has expired, so names added to those lists later are missing.

How do I find the day of a name?

Open the Search tab and type a name. Nameo shows its days in each calendar you chose. Search ignores capital letters and accents, so "Zofia" and "zofia" find the same name.

How do reminders work?

Tap Add a person in the People tab, or tap Remind me in Search. Turn on Remind me for that person. Set the reminder time in the People tab. You can also turn on Also remind me 1 day before. The free version reminds you of up to 3 people. Nameo Pro has no limit. If no reminder arrives, allow notifications for Nameo in the Settings app on your phone.

How do I add people from Contacts?

This is a Nameo Pro feature. Tap Add from Contacts in the People tab and allow access. Nameo reads first names only, finds their name days and asks you to confirm each person. Names are matched on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. Contacts access is optional.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen. Tap Edit, then tap Add Widget. Search for Nameo, then pick a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, then tap Customize. Tap Lock Screen, then tap the space under the clock and pick Nameo. The widgets show the new names at midnight without opening the app. Nameo Pro adds 2 more widget styles. In the app, Settings then Widgets shows these steps.

What does Nameo Pro unlock?

Every calendar side by side, unlimited reminders, people from Contacts, 2 more widget styles and 6 more themes, with one payment. There is no subscription. Open Settings, then tap Get Nameo Pro to see the price in your currency.

I bought Nameo Pro on another phone

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Pro unlocks on this phone at no extra cost.

Where do the name lists come from?

Each calendar follows the list most printed calendars use in that country. Most lists were checked against a second source. Wikipedia lists are by Wikipedia contributors and are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Nameo turned them into a list by day. The app shows the same list in Settings, under Name list sources.

  • Bulgaria: Имен ден, Wikipedia (bg) (CC BY-SA 4.0), checked against nameday.abalin.net (facts only, no license stated).
  • Croatia: imendan.net (facts only, no license stated), Imendani, Wikipedia (hr) (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  • Czechia: Jmeniny v Česku, Wikipedia (cs) (CC BY-SA 4.0), checked against kalendar.beda.cz (facts only, no license stated).
  • Denmark: Danske navnedage, Wikipedia (da) (CC BY-SA 4.0), checked against nameday.abalin.net (facts only, no license stated).
  • Estonia: Statistikaamet: Nimepäevad (official public data), checked against calendar.center (facts only, no license stated).
  • Finland, list of 2010: Helsingin yliopisto: Yliopiston nimipäiväalmanakka 2009 (list of 2010) (old list, protection has expired).
  • Greece: Εορτολόγιο, Πύλη:Ορθοδοξία, Wikipedia (el) (CC BY-SA 4.0), Πασχάλιος κύκλος, Wikipedia (el) (CC BY-SA 4.0), Πεντηκοστάριο, Wikipedia (el) (CC BY-SA 4.0), Άγιοι Ραφαήλ, Νικόλαος και Ειρήνη, Wikipedia (el) (CC BY-SA 4.0), Joseph and David date rule, Εθνική Έγερσις (facts only, no license stated), Saint George date rule, tanea.gr (facts only, no license stated), Saint Mark date rule, Orthodoxia News Agency (facts only, no license stated).
  • Hungary: Magyar névnapok listája, Wikipedia (hu) (CC BY-SA 4.0), checked against nevnap365.hu (facts only, no license stated).
  • Latvia: Valsts valodas centrs: Latviešu tradicionālo vārdadienu saraksts, data.gov.lv (CC0 1.0).
  • Lithuania: Behind the Name (facts only, no license stated), Vardadieniai, Wikipedia (lt) (CC BY-SA 4.0), nameday.abalin.net (facts only, no license stated).
  • Poland: kalendarzswiat.pl (facts only, no license stated), Imieniny, Wikipedia (pl) (CC BY-SA 4.0), checked against imienniczek.pl (facts only, no license stated).
  • Romania: DGEP population register, via Agerpres (official public data), Memento Calendar, ro_namedays.json (MIT), Zilele sfinților în 2022, Alba24 (facts only, no license stated).
  • Slovakia: Meniny na Slovensku, Wikipedia (sk) (CC BY-SA 4.0), checked against TASR, teraz.sk (facts only, no license stated).
  • Slovenia: Koledar svetnikov, Wikipedia (sl) (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  • Sweden, list of 2001: Svenska Akademien: Namnlängd 2001, datumordning (old list, protection has expired), checked against Lista över namnsdagar i Sverige, Wikipedia (sv), 2010 (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Can I change the language?

The app follows your phone's language. To pick another one, open Settings and tap the row under Language. The app is available in 52 languages.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.
