Privacy Policy

Your photos stay on your iPhone. Tidy Roll collects nothing.

Last updated 2026-10-23

What the app does with your photos

Tidy Roll reads your photo library on your device to find duplicate and similar photos, large videos, screenshots, Live Photos, blurry photos and bursts. Photos are never uploaded, shared or sent to a server. The app has no account and no server.

What the app stores

The app saves your settings, your swipe progress, the space you freed and how many items you deleted this week in its own storage on your device. It also keeps a small cache of photo fingerprints on your device so it does not have to check every photo again. Deleting the app removes all of it.

What the app does not do

  • No data is collected by the developer or by anyone else.
  • No analytics and no crash reporting.
  • No tracking and no advertising. The app shows no ads.
  • No third-party services receive any information from the app.

Photos access

The app asks for access to Photos so it can show and delete the items you choose. You can allow all photos or only the ones you pick. You can change this at any time in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Photos, then Tidy Roll. Without access the app cannot show your photos.

Deleting photos

When you delete items, iOS asks you to confirm and moves them to Recently Deleted in Photos. Photos keeps them for 30 days and then removes them. You can empty Recently Deleted sooner.

Purchases

Tidy Roll Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. The developer never sees your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only learns whether you own Tidy Roll Pro.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Because the data never leaves your phone, there is nothing to request from the developer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
