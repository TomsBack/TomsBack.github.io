Privacy Policy

Subscription Tracker does not collect anything.

Last updated 7 October 2026

What the app stores

The subscriptions you enter — their names, amounts, billing cycles, dates, categories and notes — are saved in the app's own storage on your device. Your currency choice and reminder settings are stored the same way.

What leaves your device

None of your subscriptions. The app has no server, no account and no analytics. It does not send your data anywhere, so there is nothing for us to see, sell or lose.

Three things do reach outside the app, and only when you tap them:

  • Manage on … and Open … open that company's own account page in a browser. What happens there is between you and them.
  • Unlock Pro and Restore purchase contact the App Store or Google Play. See Purchases below.
  • Export backup hands a JSON file to your device's share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice.

Reminders

Renewal reminders are scheduled locally by your device from the dates you entered. They are not push notifications: no message is sent from anywhere, so no server ever learns what you subscribe to or when.

Purchases

The Pro purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play. When the app opens, it also asks the store whether Pro was already bought. The app receives only that confirmation. No payment details reach the app or the developer.

Backups you make yourself

An exported backup is a plain, unencrypted JSON file containing your subscriptions. If you save it to a cloud drive or send it to yourself, it is covered by that service's privacy policy rather than this one.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. There is no copy anywhere else to request or erase.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated date above changes with it. Any version that collected data would be a different app, and would say so plainly.
