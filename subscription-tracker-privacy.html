Privacy Policy

Subscription Tracker does not collect your subscriptions.

Last updated 12 November 2026

What the app stores

The subscriptions you enter — their names, amounts, billing cycles, dates, categories and notes — are saved in the app's own storage on your device. Your currency choice and reminder settings are stored the same way.

What leaves your device

None of your subscriptions. The app has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads. It does not send your data anywhere, so there is nothing for us to see, sell or lose. On Android the app does not use the advertising ID.

Three things do reach outside the app, and only when you tap them:

  • Manage on … and Open … open that company's own account page in a browser. What happens there is between you and them.
  • Unlock Pro and Restore purchase contact the App Store or Google Play. See Purchases below.
  • Export backup hands a JSON file to your device's share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice.

Two things happen without a tap:

  • Update check. When the app opens, it asks Expo, the service that delivers app updates, whether a newer version exists. The request carries a random ID made for this install, the app version and the platform. It carries none of your subscriptions.
  • Rating prompt. After your third added subscription, the app may ask the App Store or Google Play to show its rating prompt. Your rating goes to that store.

Reminders

Renewal reminders are scheduled locally by your device from the dates you entered. They are not push notifications: no message is sent from anywhere, so no server ever learns what you subscribe to or when.

On Android 13 and later, the app asks for permission to send notifications when you turn on renewal reminders. Reminders use inexact alarms, so the app needs no exact alarm permission.

Purchases

The Pro purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play. When the app opens, it also asks the store whether Pro was already bought. The app receives only that confirmation. No payment details reach the app or the developer.

On Android, Google Play handles the purchase with the Google account on the phone. Restore purchase asks Google Play which purchases that account owns.

Backups you make yourself

An exported backup is a plain, unencrypted JSON file containing your subscriptions. If you save it to a cloud drive or send it to yourself, it is covered by that service's privacy policy rather than this one.

On Android, export uses the share sheet and restore uses the system file picker. The app has no permission to read your storage. It reads only the file you pick.

Children

The app collects none of your subscriptions or other personal details from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored, including its update check ID. Your subscriptions have no copy anywhere else to request or erase.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated date above changes with it. Any version that collected data would be a different app, and would say so plainly.
