Privacy Policy

Your pull-up data stays on your phone. BarDay collects nothing.

Last updated 8 October 2026

What the app stores

Your daily PR, attempt window, ceiling goal, every attempt and every set you log are saved in the app's own database on your device. The app has no account and no server. Your data is never uploaded.

What the app does not do

  • No data is collected by the developer or by anyone else.
  • No analytics and no crash reporting.
  • No tracking and no advertising. The app shows no ads.
  • No third-party services receive any information from the app.
  • No purchases and no payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Reset app in Profile erases your setup and history. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Because the data never leaves your phone, there is nothing to request from the developer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
