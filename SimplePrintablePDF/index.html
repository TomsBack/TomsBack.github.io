Simple Printable PDF

Simple Printable PDF is a small digital shop on Etsy. It sells printable planners, trackers and journals for cross stitch and embroidery, knitting and sewing, crafting, teaching, pen pals, recipes, plant care, gentle fitness and holiday planning. Buyers download the PDF files and print them at home in US Letter, A4 or A5.

The shop uses a private publishing tool, "Simple Printable PDF Pin Publisher". It is used only by the shop owner and does not access other people's Pinterest accounts. See the privacy policy.
