Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy covers the Simple Printable PDF website and the shop's private publishing tool, "Simple Printable PDF Pin Publisher" (the "App"). The App is operated by the owner of the Simple Printable PDF Etsy shop, Czech Republic.

What the App does

The App publishes Pins and manages boards for the shop's own Pinterest business account (pinterest.com/Tom_SimplePrintable). Each Pin shows a product image and links to the matching Etsy listing. The App also reads basic analytics for those Pins so the shop can see which products people are interested in. Only the shop owner uses the App.

Pinterest data the App uses

  • Account information of the connected account (user name and account id), to confirm which account is connected.
  • Boards and Pins of the connected account, to create boards, create and schedule Pins, and avoid duplicates.
  • Pin and account analytics (impressions, saves, Pin clicks, outbound clicks), to measure which Pins work.
  • Ad account reporting data of the connected account, read only, to compare paid and organic results.

The App requests only the OAuth scopes it needs: user_accounts:read, boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write and ads:read. It does not access other Pinterest users' data, private messages, payment details or passwords.

Content the App sends to Pinterest

Pin titles, descriptions, alt text, product images and destination links for the shop's own Etsy listings. This content is written by the shop and is meant to be public on Pinterest.

Storage and security

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the shop owner's computer, in the user's protected application data folder, and are never committed to code repositories or shared. A local log keeps the ids, titles and dates of published Pins. Nothing is stored on third party servers by the App.

Sharing

Pinterest data is not sold, rented, shared with third parties or used to train AI models. It is used only to run and measure the shop's own Pinterest presence.

Retention and deletion

Analytics snapshots are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted. Tokens are deleted when access is revoked. You can revoke the App's access at any time in Pinterest settings under Apps, or by email. Deletion requests are handled within 30 days.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking. GitHub may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Etsy purchases

Orders are placed and paid on Etsy. Etsy's privacy policy covers buyer data from purchases.
