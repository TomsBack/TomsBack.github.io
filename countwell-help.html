Countwell: Countdown Widget Help

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email us.

How do I add an event?

Tap the plus button on Home. Enter a title, pick a date, and choose all day or a time. You can repeat an event every week, month or year. After a one-time event passes, Countwell counts up the days since.

How do I add a widget?

On the Home Screen, touch and hold an empty spot, tap the plus button and search for Countwell. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, then Add Widgets. Touch and hold a widget and tap Edit Widget to pick an event or Next event. Widgets are free.

How do I use Siri?

Say or type "How many days until" and the name of your event. You can also add the shortcut in the Shortcuts app.

Why don't I get reminders?

Each event has 1 reminder, or up to 5 with Countwell Pro. Allow notifications for Countwell in the Settings app on your phone. Check that the reminder time is still in the future.

How do I back up my events?

Open Settings, tap Backup, then Export backup to save a file. To restore, tap Import backup and pick the file. You can merge it with your events or replace them. Backups never change your purchase.

What does Countwell Pro unlock?

8 more themes, 4 app icons, up to 5 reminders per event, folders, share cards and 2 more widget styles, with one payment. There is no subscription. Open Settings, then tap Get Countwell Pro to see the price in your currency.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Pro unlocks on this phone at no extra cost.

Can I change the language?

The app follows your phone's language. To pick another one, open Settings and tap Language. The app is available in 48 languages.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.
