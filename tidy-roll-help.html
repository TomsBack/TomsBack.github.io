Tidy Roll: Duplicate Photos Help

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email us.

What do the categories mean?

Duplicates are exact copies. Similar are photos of the same moment taken 2 or more times. Screenshots, Videos (largest first), Live Photos, Blurry and Bursts are listed on their own. In each group the best photo is marked "Best". Select all but the best keeps it and selects the rest.

How does swipe mode work?

Open the Swipe tab and pick a month. Swipe a photo left to delete it and right to keep it. The buttons and Undo do the same. At the end you see everything you marked and confirm the delete. Nothing is deleted before that.

How many photos can I delete for free?

50 photos or videos per week. The week runs from Monday to Sunday in your local time. Tidy Roll Pro removes the limit.

Where do deleted photos go?

To Recently Deleted in Photos. Photos keeps them for 30 days. The space comes back when Recently Deleted is emptied. To empty it sooner, open Photos, tap Albums, then Recently Deleted, tap Select, then Delete All.

Tidy Roll only sees some of my photos

You allowed access to selected photos only. Open Tidy Roll and tap Change selection, or tap Allow all photos. You can also go to Settings, Privacy & Security, Photos, Tidy Roll.

Why is a photo not in any group?

Photos stored only in iCloud and not on the phone are skipped. Open the photo in Photos to download it, then Tidy Roll checks it on the next scan.

What does Tidy Roll Pro unlock?

Unlimited deletes, Smart Select (select every suggestion in a category in one tap), Live Photos to stills (keeps the photo, drops its video part) and more themes. It is one payment with no subscription. Open Settings, then tap Get Tidy Roll Pro to see the price in your currency.

I bought Tidy Roll Pro on another phone

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Pro unlocks on this phone at no extra cost.

Can I change the language?

The app follows your phone's language. To pick another one, open Settings and tap Language. The app is available in 48 languages.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.
