Tidy Roll: Duplicate Photos Help

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email us.

What do the categories mean?

Duplicates are exact copies. Similar are photos of the same moment taken 2 or more times. Screenshots, Videos (largest first), Live Photos, Blurry and Bursts are listed on their own. In each group the best photo is marked "Best". Select all but the best keeps it and selects the rest.

Smart categories: Dark shots (very dark or blank photos, like pocket shots), Documents (photos that are mostly text, found with on-device text detection), Chat media (photos and videos saved by WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal and other chat apps), Small images (low resolution, often saved from the web), Old screen recordings and On This Day (photos taken on today's date in earlier years). Tidy Roll checks photos in batches in the background and picks up where it stopped when you reopen the app. Dark shots, Documents and Chat media need Tidy Roll Pro.

How do I merge duplicate contacts?

Open the Contacts tab and tap Allow access. Duplicates lists contacts with the same name, phone number or email. In each group tap the contact to keep, then tap Merge contacts. The kept contact gets every phone number, email, address, birthday and note from the others. Tap Undo merge right after to restore the last merge. Incomplete lists empty contacts and contacts with no name, or no phone number and no email. Select them and tap Delete. Tidy Roll never changes a contact until you tap Merge or Delete. You can merge 3 groups per week. Tidy Roll Pro removes the limit.

What does the Space tab show?

Overview shows your library size by type, by year and by source album, such as Screenshots or chat app albums. History lists what you cleaned and Plan sets a weekly goal with a streak and an optional monthly reminder. History and Plan need Tidy Roll Pro. The reminder is a local notification on your phone. Turn it on in Plan and allow notifications.

How does swipe mode work?

Open the Swipe tab and pick a month. Swipe a photo left to delete it and right to keep it. The buttons and Undo do the same. At the end you see everything you marked and confirm the delete. Nothing is deleted before that.

How many photos can I delete without Tidy Roll Pro?

50 photos or videos per week. The week runs from Monday to Sunday in your local time. Tidy Roll Pro removes the limit.

Where do deleted photos go?

To Recently Deleted in Photos. Photos keeps them for 30 days. The space comes back when Recently Deleted is emptied. To empty it sooner, open Photos, tap Albums, then Recently Deleted, tap Select, then Delete All.

Tidy Roll only sees some of my photos

You allowed access to selected photos only. Open Tidy Roll and tap Change selection, or tap Allow all photos. You can also go to Settings, Privacy & Security, Photos, Tidy Roll.

Why is a photo not in any group?

Photos stored only in iCloud and not on the phone are skipped. Open the photo in Photos to download it, then Tidy Roll checks it on the next scan.

What does Tidy Roll Pro unlock?

Unlimited deletes and contact merges, Smart Select (select every suggestion in a category in one tap), Live Photos to stills (keeps the photo, drops its video part), the Dark shots, Documents and Chat media categories, cleanup History and Plan, and more themes. Open Settings, then tap Get Tidy Roll Pro to see the price in your currency.

I bought Tidy Roll Pro on another phone

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Pro unlocks on this phone at no extra cost.

Can I change the language?

The app follows your phone's language. To pick another one, open Settings and tap Language. The app is available in 48 languages.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. The app has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.
